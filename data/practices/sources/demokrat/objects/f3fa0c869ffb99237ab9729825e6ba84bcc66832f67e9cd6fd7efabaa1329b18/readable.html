                <h1>Demokrat Parti Genel Başkanı Gültekin Uysal, &quot;15 Temmuz Demokrasi ve Milli Birlik Günü&quot; nedeniyle bir mesaj yayınladı</h1>
                <br>
                <br>
                <br>
                <p><p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><strong><span style="font-size: 14.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&ldquo;Bu acı g&uuml;n&uuml;n yıld&ouml;n&uuml;m&uuml;nde şehitlerimize rahmet ve minnet dilerken AKP i&ccedil;in akıl fikir temenni ediyoruz&rdquo;</span></strong></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; font-family: Verdana, sans-serif;">&nbsp;</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; font-family: Verdana, sans-serif;">(DP Basın Merkezi &ndash; 14 Temmuz 2021) Demokrat Parti Genel Başkanı G&uuml;ltekin Uysal,&nbsp;<strong style="font-family: Verdana, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif; font-size: 14px;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">15 Temmuz Demokrasi ve Milli Birlik G&uuml;n&uuml;</span></strong>&nbsp;dolayısıyla bir mesaj yayınladı. Uysal mesajında yaşananlardan ders alınması gerektiğini s&ouml;yleyerek &ldquo;Bu acı g&uuml;n&uuml;n yıld&ouml;n&uuml;m&uuml;nde şehitlerimize rahmet ve minnet dilerken AKP i&ccedil;in akıl fikir temenni ediyoruz&rdquo; dedi.</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;">&nbsp;</p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; font-family: Verdana, sans-serif;">&nbsp;</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">15 Temmuz&rsquo;da Millet iradesinin millet tarafından korunduğuna dikkat &ccedil;eken Genel Başkan Uysal, demokrasiden kopulmasının 15 Temmuz&rsquo;u yaşatan ter&ouml;rist hainlere zemin yarattığını ifade etti.</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&ldquo;15 Temmuz Demokrasi ve Milli Birlik G&uuml;n&uuml;&rdquo;</span></strong><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">n&uuml;n 5. yıld&ouml;n&uuml;m&uuml;nde yayınladığı mesajda demokrasi ve adaletin milletimizin dertlerinin en iyi ilacı olacağına vurgu yapan Demokrat Parti Genel Başkanı G&uuml;ltekin Uysal, şunları s&ouml;yledi:</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR; mso-bidi-font-style: italic;">&ldquo;Bir &ccedil;ete doğrudan doğruya eline silah alıp milleti katletmeye yeltendi&rdquo;</span></strong></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&ldquo;Bug&uuml;n, tarihimizin ş&uuml;phesiz en acı g&uuml;nlerinden birinin yıl d&ouml;n&uuml;m&uuml;. 15 Temmuz teşebb&uuml;s&uuml;; 27 Mayıs ve 12 Eyl&uuml;l&rsquo;&uuml;n yarattığı toplumsal ve siyasal travmadan kurtulmak i&ccedil;in yıllar boyu verilmiş emekleri adeta zayi etmiştir.</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&Uuml;lkenin g&ouml;rd&uuml;ğ&uuml; en b&uuml;y&uuml;k su&ccedil; teşekk&uuml;l&uuml;n&uuml;n alenileştiği, yıllar boyu itibar suikasti yapan bir &ccedil;etenin doğrudan doğruya eline silah alıp milleti katletmeye yeltendiği bir g&uuml;n&uuml;n yıld&ouml;n&uuml;m&uuml;d&uuml;r bug&uuml;n.</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">T&uuml;rl&uuml; operasyonlarla milletin iradesini gasp etmişlerdir. İmtiyazlar, kolaylıklar ve g&ouml;z yummalarla kamu otoritesinin kudretini, iktidarını sağlamlaştırmak adına trampa eden bir siyasal anlayışın &uuml;lkeyi getirdiği halin &ouml;zetidir durum&hellip;</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR; mso-bidi-font-style: italic;">&ldquo;Millet iradesi yine milletin kendisi tarafından korunmuştur&rdquo;</span></strong></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">250 insanımızın hainler tarafından şehit edilmiştir. 2193 insanımız teşebb&uuml;s sırasında g&ouml;vdesini memlekete siper etmiştir. 15 Temmuz&rsquo;da millet iradesi yine milletin kendisi tarafından korunmuştur.</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR; mso-bidi-font-style: italic;">&ldquo;Demokrasi i&ccedil;in didinmek yerine demokrasi ile didiştiler&rdquo;</span></strong></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></strong></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">Evveli hakkında s&ouml;ylenecek onlarca s&ouml;z var muhakkak, lakin 15 Temmuz&rsquo;a nasıl gelindiğinin cevabı, demokrasinin hali ile doğru orantılıdır.</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">Demokrasi kan kaybettik&ccedil;e 15 Temmuz&rsquo;un m&uuml;sebbibi ter&ouml;ristlerin y&uuml;z&uuml;ne kan geldiği aşikardır. Dahası demokrasiden uzaklaşıldığı i&ccedil;in, adaletten kopulduğu i&ccedil;in bu hain s&uuml;r&uuml;s&uuml;n&uuml;n alan kazandığı da bir ger&ccedil;ektir.</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&Uuml;lkenin kahir ekseriyeti demokrasi i&ccedil;in didinirken, demokrasiyi kendi iktidarı i&ccedil;in risk bilenlerin demokrasi ile, cumhuriyet değer ve kazanımları ile didişmesi 15 Temmuz&rsquo;a giden yolun taşlarını d&ouml;şemiştir.</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR; mso-bidi-font-style: italic;">&ldquo;20 yıldır yapılan her hatanın maliyetini millet &ouml;demiştir&rdquo;</span></strong></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></strong></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">D&uuml;n iktidarını muhafaza etmek i&ccedil;in demokrasi ile didişenler bug&uuml;n hepten demokrasinin canına okumaya yeltenmişlerdir.</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&ldquo;T&uuml;rkiye&rsquo;de hukuk yok/adalet işlemiyor&rdquo;</span></strong><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;dediğimiz, denetimsiz bir idari anlayışın, dengesiz/orantısız bir siyasi g&uuml;c&uuml;n varlığının sonu 15 Temmuz&rsquo;a &ccedil;ıkmıştır.</span></p>
<p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-margin-top-alt: auto; margin-bottom: .0001pt; line-height: normal;"><span style="font-size: 12.0pt; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; mso-fareast-font-family: 'Times New Roman'; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial; color: #444444; mso-fareast-language: TR;">&nbsp;</span></p>
