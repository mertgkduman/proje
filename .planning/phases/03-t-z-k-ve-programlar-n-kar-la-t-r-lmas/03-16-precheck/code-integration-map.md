## Descent and version pitfalls

- `validate_comparison_descent` (`:840–880`) enforces forward stage/contract/taxonomy, preservation of previous comparison and inventory records, lineage, and referenced evidence. Once a new completion marker exists, removal or downgrade must be rejected in both load/publish and recursive audit paths; the current version tuple only knows 1–3 (`:849`). Preserve previously persisted completion/review records as well.
- Do not manufacture a taxonomy revision to obtain completion. `comparison_reconcile` (`:427–476`) requires the immediate next taxonomy, an actual changed topic, all old topics and nine-party reviewed coverage. Contract 3 first activation is tied to that real transition (`:478–479`, `:851–858`); `validate_versioned_row_lineage` (`:1712–1787`) requires full transition edges and authored meaning decisions. Current validation 2 can receive a separately versioned completion marker without inventing lineage; future real contract-3 transitions still retain their current structured lineage validation.
- `validate_changed_topic_reviews` (`:1671–1708`) checks agent review and referenced evidence for changed topics; `validate_versioned_row_lineage` checks definitions, conditions, referenced passages and timezone-aware review. These are useful structural analogs, but neither proves that an independent substantive second reading occurred. Bind genuine review rationale and coverage to the closed snapshot and keep technical integrity distinct from semantic and human acceptance.
- Shared `missing` records may account for multiple genuinely unavailable cells; existing inventory validates the party, heading and matching search/access/verification attempt (`:703–710`). Conversely, available full text cannot be relabelled wholesale as absent. Preserve supplement/historical source roles and all relevant conditions and evidence.
