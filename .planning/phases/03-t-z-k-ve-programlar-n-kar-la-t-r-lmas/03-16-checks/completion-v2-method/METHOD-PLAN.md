## Authoritative inputs and their limits

`source-basis.json` constructs expectations before it reads a candidate. Inputs are the original `data/dossiers/02-*.json` source packages, party charter/programme packages, their authored semantic and finding reviews, original dated negative scopes, genuine missing/attempt pairs, topic source bases, source inventory and explicit taxonomy mappings/backfill judgments. `03-source-review.json` and release rows are excluded from construction. The release is read afterward only to report discrepancies in `construction-check.json`.

There are 305 source-defined topics × nine parties = **2,745 topic/party dispositions**. They contain **4,935 source-context cells**, **3,941 positively supported field obligations**, **4,838 distinct source assertions**, **854 source-authored synthesis obligations with their typed source judgments**, and **zero unresolved source origins or mapping cases**. These counts describe this corpus; no validator should use those numeric constants as coverage rules. Every cell points to an original authored judgment and source evidence or dated scope. The 4,917 unchanged mappings plus 18 authored additions/replacements are explicit. Mappings preserve research meaning and original dates; they do not create new semantic approval.

`risk-decisions.json` contains an independent universe of **49 applicable risk classes**, their selection rationale, typed witness constraints, 25 source-reading groups, 95 field inspection instances, 24 inspected syntheses, nine inspected before/after changes, 27 bounded scope/document-gap decisions, the physical DYP page-gap decision and two Saadet copy facts. The original 24 groups retain their original reading provenance. There were 94 field occurrences in those groups, including one field repeated across groups; 93 distinct inspected fields is the earlier unique count. SR25 is a separately dated new bounded original-HTML reading of a programme **principle**, a risk family missing from the original sample. It adds one distinct field inspection. No source correction was needed.

These artifacts record agent-authored source judgments and faithful reuse, not mathematical proof that every summary is true. Source hashes prove byte identity; source-linked explanations establish the recorded reasoning. The ledger does not claim a new full reading of the corpus. In particular, a field marked `not_asserted_by_this_source_judgment` means exactly that this source-context judgment does not establish an additional field. It does not mean a whole programme lacks that field, that the field is inapplicable, or that another source-context obligation may be omitted.

## CR-01: source expectations and field dispositions

Use the logical key `{topic_id, party_id, source_role, version_id, level, organ, context_judgment}`. `context_judgment` identifies the inherited source assertion/context, distinguishing two differently conditioned assertions from the same source. `taxonomy_version` belongs to the enclosing basis. A cell ID is a stable canonical digest of this key. `fulfilment_locator_only` is a migration aid for locating the present row; it is not the authority for completeness. A new valid row ID may fulfil the same logical obligation when its evidence, meaning, dimensions and explicitly recorded mapping agree.

For each topic/party pair, require the complete set of source-context dispositions in its trusted basis. Require its applicable source roles, versions, levels and organs; do not expand prose such as `ilgili organ ve düzeyler` into a fabricated Cartesian product. Conversely, that prose must never authorize dropping an original source-scoped organ. No source-backed inapplicability judgment was required to construct this corpus: the existing corpus closes with positive content, explicit bounded absence or real documentary gaps. Do not invent `inapplicable` entries to make an absent candidate pass. A future such disposition needs its own cited full relevant source boundary and explicit authored reason.

For every positive field assertion, require the stated field family, condition, original summary/quote finding pair, all cited supporting/context passages, correct version/party/source role and genuine semantic-review lineage. Preserve multiple field assertions of the same family when they carry different conditions. Other programme families receive the recorded **local** disposition, rather than a forced positive value. At topic/party level, union all source contexts before deciding what the reader knows. If only a target is established in a context, no instrument may be invented. If another context establishes an instrument, the local non-assertion cannot suppress it. Negative scopes must retain their actual sections/query/time/reason; documentary gaps must retain their genuine missing→attempt chains. A single real document search can close many topic cells.

The inventory obligations come from original `03-source-inventory-v4.json` sections, not the candidate's inventory count. Match canonical section content and source ownership; preserve its search boundary and unmatched-heading dispositions. A scope can establish a topic absent from a section's `topic_ids`; never substitute inventory membership for a dated source judgment.

Require every source-authored synthesis obligation, including its separate conditional/negative sides, rather than merely one surviving synthesis per topic. These obligations come from the original authored synthesis/semantic packages, not the candidate completion proof. A newly authored wording may fulfil a semantic obligation only through an explicit source-backed supersession/review; it may not silently erase a qualified source claim.

Concrete TİP requirement: topic `aday-tanitimi`, party `tip`, role `charter`, version `0203-tip-charter-v1`, level `ulusal ve yerel`, organ `Kamu adayı tanıtımı`, disposition `no_explicit_statement`. Authority is scope `0304-tip-charter-scope-aday-tanitim-html-scope`, reviewed `2026-11-13T13:55:32.558426+00:00`, sections `HTML tüzük Bölüm 1–7, Madde 1.1–7.2`, query `Kamu adayı tanıtımı`. The authored reason distinguishes PM's candidate-selection power from a separate candidate-introduction procedure. The present fulfilment row is `04-03-v3-0304-tip-charter-scope-aday-tanitim-html-row`. It is required even though TİP inventory does not directly list this topic. Deleting that row, the candidate proof row and a count cannot discharge this independent obligation. Deleting the stored obligation as well is a forbidden mutation of its adopted basis.

## CR-02: typed substantive review coverage

The 49 expected risks are persisted independently of the submitted sample. Applicability comes from locked D-01–D-16 decisions and the source corpus, including source roles/formats, field families, centre/province/district/other organs, rights/appeals, conditions, multiple passages, uncertain alternatives, before/after, historical identity, supplemental content with programme gaps, genuine missing documents, narrow negatives, minority topics, manual/image locators, physical page gaps, duplicate copies, proposal/body distinctions and three synthesis claim types.

Validate each witness against the risk's `witness_constraints`, the source-basis cell/assertion and the actual records. A field witness must identify the inspected field, both findings, its supporting passages, version, role, decision, source-reading scope and reason. A synthesis witness must identify the actual synthesis and typed claim, every positive side's own passages, every negative side's actual scope and every documentary side's missing/attempt chain. Verify all linked content and ownership, not merely nonempty text or a shared party name. A before/after witness must link an actual change and two genuine distinct versions/passages. Scope and missing witnesses may have zero fields, but cannot satisfy a field-family risk. A physical-gap decision does not need a comparison row. Copy-equality facts do not substitute for source→summary decisions.

Require coverage of every applicable risk class, without requiring 24/25 groups, 93/94 fields, a percentage or any other sample quota. One substantive decision can cover multiple compatible risks. Reused old meaning decisions retain their original times and limits. A newly authored alternative witness requires a new immutable review revision, not edits to the expected risk universe to fit a reduced sample. Deleting all field and synthesis inspections while retaining one row per party must leave field/synthesis risks uncovered and fail through all entry points. The former free-text-only source-review shape is retained only inside historical contract-1 proof interpretation.

Examples already present: SR13 keeps Saadet's birth/work/tax conditions distinct from Demokrat's every-citizen cash-income wording; SR18 keeps DEM's needs threshold distinct from EMEP's proposal for sufficient free water, and retains both contextual/support passages; SR21 retains the BAU emissions baseline and the two-page import-carbon-tax condition. SR25's TİP programme principle retains both multidimensional crisis analysis and the explicit rejection of automatic collapse; no amount, timetable or administrative instrument is inferred. The 24 actual synthesis decisions include all three typed categories; membership in a case group alone is insufficient to prove a particular synthesis was inspected.

## CR-03: append-only basis and completion revisions

Keep `comparison_completion` and its current `(version,taxonomy_version)` primary key unchanged for historical contract-1 markers. Add separate tables; do not put contract-2 rows into that table and do not add an `active` boolean that must later be rewritten.

| `comparison_coverage_disposition` | `(basis_id,cell_id)` PK, basis FK; `logical_key`, `disposition`, `source_judgment`, `mapping_refs`, `basis_ids`, `field_dispositions` as canonical JSON/text. These are source obligations, not candidate snapshots. |

The highest epoch is the active immutable revision. Require a single connected parent chain, increasing epochs, supported versions, valid timestamps and no competing heads. Active taxonomy/stage must match `comparison_contract`. Bind `selection` to the selected rows/fields/syntheses/inventory and their exact canonical record contents or immutable identities plus digests; this is the **candidate snapshot binding**, separate from independent expectations. All existing selected source obligations must be fulfilled, all candidate additions need a source-backed disposition, and all active topic/party pairs need their source-backed synthesis coverage. Neither an unbound candidate row nor an expectation erased from a new basis is acceptable. A new basis preserves the previous obligations or supplies explicit source-backed supersession; all prior basis/revision rows remain byte-equivalent.

For promotion, store both `source_assertions` and `synthesis_obligations` in the basis record's structured `source_assertions` JSON object under distinct named keys. Freeze the actual referenced original source judgments and mapping/definition metadata with the basis, not just mutable research-file paths. Paths/SHA/pointers provide provenance; a future audit must be able to validate the retained assertion without relying on today's mutable `data/comparisons` files. The supplied source assertions already retain the dated scope, missing/attempt and semantic-review bodies. Include the referenced original logical-dimension/mapping/topic metadata when serializing the importer basis. On first adoption, resolve every provenance pointer against its recorded source-package digest and reconcile the entire originating judgment universe; reject an initial pruned basis, not just pruning after adoption. `build_source_basis.py` specifies the deterministic source-only reconciliation. The root/executor's review of this separately authored expectation artifact is the trust point; no hash claims to perform semantic review and no per-source human approval is introduced.

Historical contract-1 validation has two modes: on its own original closed release, preserve the original interpreter and binding; retained inside a later revision, validate its frozen projection from the marker's five record sets, requiring every historical record to remain present and unchanged, with its original source-review interpretation. Do not compare that historical marker to every newly added same-taxonomy row. Also validate every retained contract-2 revision against its own frozen selection, basis and review. Active complete validation is additional. Partial active revisions retain and validate old complete proofs but do not claim that the new active selection is complete.

Do not permit arbitrary mutable inputs to select the weak legacy interpreter. Existing closed contract-1 snapshots remain valid; a forward package/release from a marked base that changes comparison content, basis, review or stage must carry the new revision contract. `validate_completion` may resolve legacy context from the actual release/manifest or accept an explicit verified historical context from callers; a string `release_id` by itself is not authentication. A modified object claiming the accepted release ID must not inherit its historical exemption. A direct call on the exact original accepted data remains valid. Unknown or dropped adopted revisions fail, including dropping all new tables to attempt fallback.

Supported transitions:

| Base → candidate | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Historical complete → same-taxonomy complete | Append contract-2 basis/revision and bind active closure; preserve old marker. No taxonomy bump. |
| Complete → partial | Append a higher epoch with an explicit forward-work reason and selected research scope. Active contract/manifest say `partial`; historical complete proof stays immutable. A bare stage downgrade without a new revision is rejected. |
| Partial → complete | Append a higher complete revision after every applicable source/field/risk obligation is discharged; prior partial and complete revisions remain immutable. |
| Same-taxonomy sourced addition | Append source judgment/disposition and row/field/evidence/review; append basis/revision. Earlier selected records and proofs remain intact. |
| Genuine source correction/supersession | Append new content and explicit source-backed old→new relation. Retain old records/proofs; active selection excludes an old record only through that relation. Run uniqueness and completeness on the active projection, while individually validating retained historical records. |
| New taxonomy | Use existing explicit taxonomy reconciliation/lineage rules. The active revision selects the new taxonomy and preserves prior proof interpretation. This bugfix must not trigger a new taxonomy by itself. |
